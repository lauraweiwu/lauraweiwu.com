## UC Berkeley

Undergraduate. Spring 2026.

The nature of real property; market analysis; construction cycles; mortgage lending; equity investment; metropolitan growth; urban land use; real property valuation; public policies.

Ph.D. seminar. Spring 2026.

Doctoral real estate seminar covering topics related to real estate investment, finance, and market analysis. The course applies financial and economic analysis to real estate finance, urban real estate economics, and real estate evaluation.
